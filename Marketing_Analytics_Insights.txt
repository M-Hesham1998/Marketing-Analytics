# Marketing Analytics — Key Insights

## 🔹 Marketing Performance — June 2026

* **GMV reached $2.35M**, down **3% vs. the previous month**, while **Marketing Cost decreased by 5%** to $65.59K.
* The decrease in marketing cost was greater than the decline in GMV, which helped **ROAS improve by 2% to 35.82**.
* Despite the improvement in ROAS, **CAC increased by 458% to $6,559**, indicating a significant increase in customer acquisition cost.
* **ROI reached 3,482%**, reflecting a very high return relative to marketing spend.
* The GMV trend shows **strong growth throughout early 2026**, reaching high levels in May before a noticeable decline in June.

---

## 🔹 Channel & Campaign Performance — July 2026

* **Paid Channels** accounted for most of the marketing spend, at approximately **$41.97K**, compared with only **$1.34K for Owned Channels**.
* Despite the lower spend, **Owned Channels achieved a CTR of 20.14% vs. 2.43% for Paid Channels**, and generated **4,239 conversions vs. 1,437**.
* This indicates that **Owned Channels delivered significantly higher engagement and conversion efficiency despite much lower spending**.
* At the campaign level, **HighValue_Upsell** generated the highest number of conversions at approximately **1,015**, followed by **AtRisk_Reactivation with 981**.
* **AtRisk_Reactivation achieved an 8% CVR**, the highest among the displayed campaigns, while HighValue_Upsell achieved a **21.42% CTR and 7.63% CVR**.

## 🔹 Funnel & Customer Journey — All Data

* The funnel starts with approximately **40M impressions** and drops to around **4M Engagement**, making **Impressions → Engagement** the largest drop-off point.
* **Sessions reached 3.9M**, which is relatively close to the Engagement volume, indicating a more stable transition from Engagement to Sessions.
* The final displayed **GMV is $9.9M**, with the funnel showing **24.7%** relative to the impressions value used as the chart reference.
* The Customer Journey includes approximately **800 Active Merchants**, including **624 Repeat Buyers**, while **484 are classified as Risk Merchants**.
* The strong number of Repeat Buyers is a positive indicator of customer continuity, while the high number of Risk Merchants represents an important retention opportunity.

---

## 🔹 Personas, Segments & Markets — All Data

* **Omar the Ops Manager** generated the highest GMV among the displayed personas, at approximately **$2.54M**, despite having fewer merchants than some other personas.
* **Layla the CFO** had the highest **Average LTV at approximately $42.97K**, followed by **Nour the Enterprise Buyer at approximately $38.81K**. This shows that merchant volume is not the only factor driving persona value.
* **Enterprise** was the largest segment by GMV, generating approximately **$6.50M** compared with **$3.41M for SME**, representing around **65.6% of total GMV**.
* **United Arab Emirates** achieved the highest ROAS among the displayed markets, followed by **Saudi Arabia and Egypt**.
* The merchant base consists of **458 B2C and 342 B2B merchants**, meaning **B2B represents approximately 42.75%** of the total merchant base.
* Enterprise contributes a significantly larger share of GMV relative to its merchant base, indicating **higher value per merchant within the Enterprise segment**.
